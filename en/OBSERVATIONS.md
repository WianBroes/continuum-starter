# OBSERVATIONS — raw buffer

> Written by appending only, by the merge (`## Feedback` section of closed sessions). Never rewritten, except to set a marker during consolidation (`protocols/learning.md`).
> Format: `**[YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM]** [type] — text`. Types: `[incident]` (exact quote), `[success]`, `[gap]`, `[lesson]` (« When … → do … »), `[fact]`; plus `[AI]` for an interpretation without a quote (never promoted on its own).
> Markers set during consolidation, on the entry's `**[date]**` line: `[proposed → DIRECTIVES.md]` (became a directive), `[graduated]` (became a trait or a profile fact), `[dismissed: reason]` (not an observation, or withdrawn on objection). An entry without a marker counts as unconsolidated; at the threshold, the loop starts by itself at close.
