# TIPS — one per startup

> Not rules: nothing here changes the agent's behavior. At each session opening, `session.sh open` shows the next tip (n = number of sessions already opened; the first session has none — it has its welcome, `protocols/first-session.md`); the agent passes it on in one line, in the user's language. List exhausted → no more tips: the system is broken in.
> Format: one line per tip, `N. text`, numbered without gaps (the script reads line N). To add one: at the end, next number.

## Tips

1. Say « close » when you're done: what was learned goes into memory, nothing is lost.
2. « From now on… »: the rule applies right away, and I keep it for next time.
3. When I tell you what I've learned and it bothers you, say « remove that »: it's removed and won't come back.
4. Pitfall — taking the AI at its word: an AI produces what sounds plausible, not necessarily what's true. Ask « where did you get that? » or « check it ».
5. « Why are you doing that? »: I'll quote the rule that guided me.
6. Pitfall — « done » without proof: a success announced is not a success observed. Ask « show me », or try it yourself.
7. « What have you learned? »: I'll sum up what I've noted, what I've deduced from it and what I apply.
8. Pitfall — the AI that says yes: AIs tend to agree with you. Ask « contradict me » or « what's the weak point? ».
9. Pitfall — exposed secret: never paste an API key or a password into the conversation; if one was, revoke it and create a new one.
10. Pitfall — the never-ending session: when a topic is done, « close » and start again. A conversation that's too long costs more and the AI forgets its beginning; the memory is in the files.
11. Pitfall — « remember » without writing: from one conversation to the next, I only keep what is written. Say « write it down ».
12. Pitfall — the fix loop: at the third failed attempt on the same problem, say « stop, what do we actually know? ».
13. For a vague or too-big request: say the expected result, and how we'll know it worked.
14. Pitfall — delegating the irreversible: sending, publishing, paying, deleting — I prepare, you review, and you make the final move.
15. Pitfall — working without a net: before a big change, ask for a backup (a commit); tests are done on copies, never on your real data.
16. One project = one numbered folder at the root (`01_MyProject/`): I keep the work there, separate from the memory.
17. Already have a memory elsewhere (another AI assistant, notes)? Say so, I can bring it here.
18. You can open several agents at the same time in this folder, in different terminals: each has its own session, they don't step on each other.
19. Pitfall — hidden instructions: a web page, an email or a PDF can contain instructions aimed at the AI. I treat them as content, never as orders.
20. Pitfall — other people's data: write only what's needed about other people, anonymize (« a colleague », « X »).
21. Pitfall — delegating judgment: for health, law or money, cross-check my answer with an official source or a professional.
22. Pitfall — accepting what you don't understand: ask « how do I know it works? ».
23. Everything is in text files you can open: `PROFILE.md` (what you've said about yourself), `DIRECTIVES.md` (my rules), `OBSERVATIONS.md` (my notes).
24. « Run the learning »: I reread my notes right away, instead of waiting until there are enough.
25. Last tip: the system is broken in, there won't be any more. « What have you learned? » shows you where it stands.
